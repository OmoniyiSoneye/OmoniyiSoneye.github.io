LeBuckets

AI basketball training app that watches you play through a camera and breaks down your game in real time.

  • Custom detector trained on 50,000+ labeled images (hoops, players, balls, nets, backboards) at 85%+ IOU
  • Real-time YOLO11 detection at 30 FPS on CPU
  • RTMPose pipeline for shooting form feedback
  • 40% lower inference latency through quantization
  • Court boundary detection and violation tracking

The whole pipeline, from data prep to training to real-time inference, is written in Python.

Fine-tuned and quantized the detection models in PyTorch, cutting inference latency 40%.

YOLO11 powers real-time detection of hoops, players, balls, nets, and backboards at 30 FPS on CPU.

OpenCV handles the video stream and the court boundary and violation detection.

Combines object detection and pose estimation to understand what's happening on court.

RTMPose tracks body keypoints to analyze shooting form and give feedback.

Roboflow was used for the 50,000+ image training dataset.

MosaicXR

Turns regular 2D photos into 3D you can view in VR on the Meta Quest 3.

  • Generated depth maps with MiDaS (PyTorch), then shifted them geometrically to build a stereo image pair
  • Web app for 2D → 3D conversion
  • Interactive Unity gallery for viewing the results in VR

The image processing backend is written in Python.

Ran the MiDaS depth estimation model in PyTorch to get a depth map from a single photo.

Used monocular depth estimation plus geometric shifting to synthesize a left/right stereo pair.

Built the VR gallery in Unity for Meta Quest 3.

The upload and convert web app uses JavaScript.

Web app frontend built with HTML/CSS.

Dynamic Anchors

A closed-loop controller that moves a camera based on plain-language instructions in fast-moving basketball scenes.

  • Evaluated across 12,000 episodes and 1.2M frames
  • Instruction satisfaction up from 0.302 (static baseline) to 1.049 in 2D and 0.962 in 3D
  • Benchmarked 124 command families in Blender-generated scenes with 7D camera actions

Controller and evaluation written in Python.

Combines language grounding, 3D perception, and vision-language-action for camera control.

Test environments were generated in Blender.
